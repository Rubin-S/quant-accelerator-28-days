# Kill session protocol (Day 10)

**Format:** for each card, I attack and you defend. 5 minutes per card, timed. Write it down.
You can't defend with "the backtest looks good": 2019+ is locked and 2007–2018 is exploration only.

## The 12 attacks (I'll use any of them)
1. **Who pays?** Name the person on the other side and why they keep paying. "Behavioural" alone isn't an answer.
2. **Why hasn't it been arbitraged?** What stops a bigger, faster player from taking it?
3. **Rules:** does it need F&O, shorting, margin or leverage above 1.0x? Then it's dead.
4. **Costs:** what's the edge per trade after the haircut vs the Day 7 round-trip cost? Show the number.
5. **Tax:** what's left after 20.8% short-term tax (20% plus cess) on every winning exit?
6. **Turnover:** how many trades a year? Every trade is a toll booth.
7. **Parameters:** how many knobs? Which one would you be tempted to tune?
8. **Sample:** how many independent bets are in 2007–2018? (Monthly rule, 12 years: about 144, and fewer for slow signals.)
9. **Evidence so far:** what did Day 8 say? Same sign? What's t, and how many trials have you run?
10. **Testability:** discovery or confirm mode? If discovery, how many years to reach t = 3?
11. **Regime:** when does it lose? (2008, the 2013 taper, 2020 COVID.) Would you keep running it through that?
12. **Falsifier and kill date:** what exact result kills it, and by when?

## Scoring
- **KILL:** fails 3, or has no payer, or costs win.
- **PARK:** sound idea, but untestable or too thin. Write what would un-park it.
- **SURVIVE:** passes all 12 attacks with numbers, not adjectives.
Expect about 3 of 10 to survive. If 7 survive, we weren't hard enough.
