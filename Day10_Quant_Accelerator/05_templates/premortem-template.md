# Pre-mortem (Gary Klein, HBR 2007; Kahneman's favourite de-biasing tool)
Do this for each SURVIVOR only. 10 minutes each.

**Setup:** It's 27 May 2027. This strategy ran live at small size for six months. It was a disaster.

1. Write the story of the disaster in 5 sentences. Be specific (dates, numbers, what you did).
2. List every cause you can think of in 3 minutes. Then sort them into:
   - Edge was never real (overfit, luck, data error, survivorship)
   - Edge was real but costs, tax or slippage ate it
   - Edge was real but decayed (crowding, regime change)
   - Plumbing failed (data feed, order bug, API/2FA, missed rebalance)
   - **I** failed (overrode the system, sized up after wins, stopped after losses)
3. For the top 3 causes: what check, rule or monitor goes into the pre-registration (Day 12) or rulebook (Day 21) to catch it?
4. Does the story change your verdict? (Yes is allowed.)
