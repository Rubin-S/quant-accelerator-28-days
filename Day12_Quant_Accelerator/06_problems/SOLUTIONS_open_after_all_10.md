3. "KILL if holdout vol ratio > 0.87." By construction a true overlay passes about 90% of the time (assuming the future resembles 2008–2018: a caveat to write down). *Size falsifiers from the null and the alternative, not from gut feel.*
4. A monthly trend filter makes about 10–20 switches. Its daily return difference against buy & hold is noisy and averages slightly negative, because it gives up some upside. Return-alpha is simply not what it delivers. *The bootstrap is telling you which hypothesis is testable.*
