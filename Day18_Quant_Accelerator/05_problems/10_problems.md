# Day 18: 10 problems
1. Re-picking each January (expanding) gave Sharpe −0.19 over 2011–2018; fixed 10m gave −0.24. Does that justify switching to "WF-selected"? What else would you need to see?
2. The expanding scheme picked 8m every year. Two readings: "8m is truly best" vs "early-sample luck locked in". How would you tell them apart without touching 2019+?
3. Walk-forward efficiency came out ~0.38 (expanding) and ~0.43 (rolling). Pardo treats ~0.5+ as healthy. Why might a low WFE here say more about regime change than about overfitting?
4. Start-date stability: the vol ratio stays 0.69 to 0.84 for every start from Sep 2008 to Dec 2009, but the drawdown edge collapses from +18 pp to +2 pp once the start passes Oct 2008. Write the one-sentence consequence for your pre-registered falsifiers.
5. The SPA "consistent" p-value is ~0.91 for "the best of 8/10/12 beats buy & hold on excess returns". State the null, the alternative, and what 0.91 means in plain words.
6. Plateau: 10m ranks 4th of 9 on full-period Sharpe; neighbours range 0.05 to 0.22. Plateau or spike? Why is "middle of a plateau" a GOOD place for a primary?
7. 2009 shows −31 pp vs buy & hold for 8–11m and −58 pp for 12–14m. Explain the mechanism (what was the filter doing in March to May 2009?).
8. Why must the walk-forward harness run on the SAME aligned start for all variants? What breaks if 14m starts later than 8m?
9. Your switch cost is 13.9 bps × |Δw|. Name two costs the WF harness still ignores, and say which direction each biases the WF result.
10. You ran 9 lookbacks + 2 WF schemes today. Update your trial count. Does the neighbourhood scan count toward the DSR N for the primary? Defend your answer in two lines.
