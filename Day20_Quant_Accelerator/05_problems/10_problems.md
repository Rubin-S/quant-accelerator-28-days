# Day 20: 10 problems (do 1–5 BEFORE sealing, 6–10 after the report)
1. McLean-Pontiff: −26% OOS; Falck et al.: ~50% kept; Suhonen et al.: −73% live. Why are these so different? Which base rate fits a simple 1-parameter trend overlay?
2. Your IS Sharpe is ~0.18 with a 90% CI roughly −0.3 to +0.6. Over ~8 holdout years, what's a sensible 90% interval for the holdout Sharpe? (Hint: SE ≈ 1/√years for a Sharpe.)
3. DSR: with N = 25 trials and a trial-Sharpe std of 0.15, the expected best-of-N Sharpe is ≈ 0.30. Your IS Sharpe is 0.18. What does DSR say before you even open the holdout?
4. Give one reason a monthly filter might do BETTER on drawdown in 2019–2026 than you expect, and one reason it might do worse.
5. Write the exact sentence you'll publish if the vol falsifier survives and the DD falsifier fails.
6. Your 90% intervals caught k of 6. If you were perfectly calibrated, what's the probability of catching all 6? Of catching 4 or fewer?
7. The holdout Sharpe beat your point prediction. Is that evidence you were right? What would "being right" look like for a probabilistic forecast?
8. Holdout ÷ IS Sharpe came out above 1. Name two explanations that are not "the strategy got better".
9. A friend says "just re-run it on NIFTYBEES instead of the index, and with 9 months, to double-check." Which of those is allowed and which is fraud? Why?
10. The holdout is spent. Where does your next untouched data come from, and how long until it says anything (use the SE from Q2)?
