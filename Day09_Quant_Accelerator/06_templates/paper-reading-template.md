## Pass 2: the trader's questions
1. Exact rule, as code-like pseudocode:
2. Data: market, instruments, period, frequency. Survivorship-free? Dividends included?
3. In-sample vs out-of-sample: where's the split? Any real post-publication period?
4. Headline result (return, vol, Sharpe, drawdown, t-stat) and what it's compared against:
5. Costs and taxes modelled? How?
6. How many variants did the authors try (stated or implied)? Haircut it (Day 5).
7. Who pays, according to the authors? Do you buy it?
8. When did it fail (worst period)?
9. Does it fit OUR rules (NSE cash ETFs/index, long/flat, 1.0x, no F&O)? If not, what's the nearest version that does?
10. One-sentence verdict + idea card #?
