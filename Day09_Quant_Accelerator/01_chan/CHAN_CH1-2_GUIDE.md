# Chan, *Quantitative Trading* (2nd ed., Wiley 2021): how to read chapters 1–2 today

**Chapter 1 ("The Whats, Whos, and Whys of Quantitative Trading")** is the publisher's free excerpt in this folder (10 pages).
**Chapter 2 ("Fishing for Ideas")** is not freely available. Legal ways to read it:
1. Your college library (NIT Puducherry), or an inter-library request.
2. O'Reilly Learning, if your institution gives access (the book is on O'Reilly).
3. Buy it (the 2nd edition, or the 2026 revised 2nd edition).
Please don't use pirated PDFs floating around: we're building a business on rules, and that starts here.

## Chapter 2's structure (from the publisher's table of contents in this folder)
**How to identify a strategy that suits YOU:** your working hours · your programming skills · your trading capital · your goal.
**A taste for plausible strategies and their pitfalls:**
1. How does it compare with a benchmark, and how consistent are its returns?
2. How deep and long is the drawdown?
3. How will transaction costs affect it?
4. Does the data suffer from survivorship bias?
5. How did the strategy's performance change over the years?
6. Does it suffer from data-snooping bias?
7. Does it "fly under the radar" of institutional money managers?
