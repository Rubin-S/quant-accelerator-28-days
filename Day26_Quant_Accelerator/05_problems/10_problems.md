6. Given (5), how should the go/no-go memo word the slippage criterion so it's honest?
7. Residual is +0.000% in the demo. What would a residual of −0.4% most likely mean?
8. Frazzini et al. found real costs far below earlier estimates. Why can't you borrow their numbers for NIFTYBEES at ₹20,000?
