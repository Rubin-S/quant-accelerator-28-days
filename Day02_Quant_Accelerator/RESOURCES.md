# Day 2 resources: every link, sorted by type
All free. ✅ = PDF already in this ZIP. Links checked on 31 Oct 2026.

## 1. Core probability (folder 01)
- ✅ Jane Street, *Probability & Markets* guide (13 pp.): the trading-firm view of EV and market making. https://www.janestreet.com/probability-markets/
- ✅ MIT 18.05 (Orloff & Bloom), all probability readings in one PDF. Course home, with slides, problem sets and solutions: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-05-introduction-to-probability-and-statistics-spring-2022/
- ✅ Stat 110 Probability Cheatsheet (Chen & Blitzstein, 10 pp.). Print it. https://github.com/wzchen/probability_cheatsheet
- ✅ Grinstead & Snell, *Introduction to Probability* (free, GNU licence). Reference: Ch. 4 Conditional Probability, Ch. 6 Expected Value & Variance, Ch. 8 Law of Large Numbers.
- Blitzstein & Hwang, *Introduction to Probability* (2nd ed.), free online from the authors (Google Drive link, couldn't be bundled): https://probabilitybook.net/
- Stat 110 Strategic Practice problems with full solutions (do #2 conditional probability and #4 expectation): https://stat110.hsites.harvard.edu/strategic-practice-problems

## 2. Bayes and base rates (folder 02)
- ✅ Ioannidis (2005), "Why Most Published Research Findings Are False", PLoS Medicine
- ✅ Gigerenzer, Gaissmaier, Kurz-Milcke, Schwartz & Woloshin (2007), "Helping Doctors and Patients Make Sense of Health Statistics" (natural frequencies)

## 3. Small-sample traps (folder 03)
- ✅ Tversky & Kahneman (1971), "Belief in the Law of Small Numbers", Psychological Bulletin
- ✅ Tversky & Kahneman (1974), "Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases", Science
- ✅ Miller & Sanjurjo (2018), "Surprised by the Hot Hand Fallacy? A Truth in the Law of Small Numbers", Econometrica (arXiv version)
- ✅ Lo (2002), "The Statistics of Sharpe Ratios", Financial Analysts Journal
