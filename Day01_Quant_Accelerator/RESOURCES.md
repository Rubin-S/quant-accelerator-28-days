## 1. Regulator studies (India) — the hard numbers
- ✅ SEBI (Sep 2024) – Profits & losses in equity derivatives FY22–FY24 (93% of individuals lost): https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/research/sep-2024/study-analysis-of-profits-and-losses-in-the-equity-derivatives-segment-fy22-fy24-_86905.html
- ✅ SEBI (Jul 2024) – Intraday trading by individuals in equity cash (71% lost; 80% with 500+ trades): https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/research/jul-2024/study-analysis-of-intraday-trading-by-individuals-in-equity-cash-segment_84946.html
- ✅ SEBI (Jul 2025) – Derivatives vs cash after new measures (FY25: ~91% lost, Rs 1,05,603 cr net loss): https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/jul-2025/1751900271726.pdf
- ✅ SEBI (Jan 2023) – First F&O study, FY22 (89% lost): https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/research/jan-2023/study-analysis-of-profit-and-loss-of-individual-traders-dealing-in-equity-fando-segment_67525.html

## 2. Research papers — why retail loses
- ✅ Barber & Odean (2000), "Trading Is Hazardous to Your Wealth", Journal of Finance
- ✅ Barber, Lee, Liu & Odean (2009), "Just How Much Do Individual Investors Lose by Trading?", Review of Financial Studies
- ✅ Barber, Lee, Liu & Odean (2004), "Do Individual Day Traders Make Money? Evidence from Taiwan"
- ✅ Barber, Lee, Liu, Odean & Zhang, "Do Day Traders Rationally Learn About Their Ability?" (published as "Learning, Fast or Slow", 2020)
