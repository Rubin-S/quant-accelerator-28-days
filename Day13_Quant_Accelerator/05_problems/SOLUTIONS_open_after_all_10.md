# Solutions
1. A 10:1 face-value split, ex-date 19 Dec 2019 (NSE circular NSE/CML/42873, dated 11 Dec 2019). Yahoo back-adjusted the history (÷10) but also divided the 19–20 Dec rows, so they're double-adjusted. *Primary source: the exchange circular. Vendor pages are leads.*
2. You don't know the vendor's error model. Multiplying by 10 "fixes" it only if your guess is right. NaN + log + a source to re-pull (NSE bhavcopy for those dates) is honest. Silent fills create fake returns. *Never repair data with a guess you can't verify.*
3. No dividend adjustment. In 2009–2018 the ETF CAGR is 14.04% price-only vs 14.25% (strict) to 14.75% (all claimed dividends). That's 0.2–0.7%/yr, which matters when your falsifier is a return give-up of a few %/yr. *Small biases compound into wrong verdicts.*
4. Strongest to weakest: NSE corporate-action filing / AMC IDCW record (primary), then the price-gap test (independent evidence), then a vendor list (secondary, often copied). *Triangulate. Two vendors agreeing may just be one copying the other.*
5. (a) Asynchronous closes: the ETF's last trade vs the index close, plus tracking noise (sd ≈ 0.72%/day), can hide a 1% drop. (b) A wrong ex-date (the test looks at the wrong day), or the amount is per a different unit basis. Also possible: there was no dividend. *A failed test means "not confirmed", not "false".*
6. Real. 28 Feb 2015 was a Saturday Budget session; 14 Nov 2020 was Muhurat trading (Diwali). Check NSE circulars and the holiday calendar. Also note NIFTY's Yahoo file lacks some of these sessions: sources disagree on the calendar. *Your calendar is data too. Audit it.*
7. Holidays: 2 Oct (Gandhi Jayanti), 3 Oct (Dussehra), the weekend, and 6 Oct 2014 (Bakri Id). Confirm with the NSE holiday list. *Not every gap is missing data.*
8. 16:00 ET = 01:30/02:30 IST the next day, so it's first usable in the next NSE session. Same-date correlation mixes in information that arrives after NSE closes. The tradable version is SPX(t−1) → NIFTY(t): +0.22. *Align by when information becomes known, not by calendar label.*
9. Parquet keeps types and timezones, is compact and fast, and carries metadata. The SHA-256 proves which raw file produced which result. The manifest records every fix. CSVs lose dtypes/timezones and invite silent edits. *Reproducibility is a property you build, not hope for.*
10. (a) NIFTY 50 TRI instead of the price index: adds about 1.2–1.5%/yr of dividends (the index yield was 1.28% at 31 Dec 2025 per the Nippon product note), which mostly accrue while you're invested. Source: niftyindices TRI. (b) A real cash leg instead of 6%: LIQUIDBEES / overnight-rate history (NSE + AMC IDCW data, or RBI rates via jugaad-data). Both move the measured "give-up" and must be fixed BEFORE Day 20. If you change them after locking, that's a v2 pre-reg. *Upgrade the data now, while you're still blind.*
