# NSE cash-market cost stack: official rates snapshot (checked 31 Oct 2026)
Every line points to its source. Re-check before you rely on it: these rates changed on 1 Oct 2024, 1 Mar 2026 and 1 Apr 2026.

| Charge | Rate | Side | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| STT: equity shares, delivery | 0.1% | buy AND sell | NSE "SEBI Turnover Fees, STT and Other levies" page (updated 17 Apr 2026); BSE Master Circular Apr 2026 (in this folder) |
| STT: equity-oriented fund/ETF units, delivery | NIL on buy; 0.001% on sell | sell | same |
| STT: non-delivery (intraday) sale of shares or equity-fund units | 0.025% | sell | same |
| STT: non-equity ETFs (e.g. gold ETF) | none (not an "equity share" or "equity-oriented fund") | n/a | the STT table covers only equity shares, equity-oriented funds and business trusts |
| NSE transaction charge (incl. IPFT) | ₹306.99 + ₹0.01 = ₹307 per crore (0.00307%) | each side | NSE/FA/73061, 27 Feb 2026, effective 1 Mar 2026 (in this folder). Before: ₹297 + ₹10 (NSE/FA/64232) |
| SEBI turnover fee | ₹10 per crore (0.0001%) | each side | NSE levies page |
| Stamp duty: delivery | 0.015% | buy | Indian Stamp Act as amended by Finance Act 2019, effective 1 Jul 2020; SEBI FAQ (in this folder); NSE levies page |
| Stamp duty: non-delivery (intraday) | 0.003% | buy | same |
| GST | 18% of (brokerage + exchange txn charge + SEBI fee) | both | NSE levies page (18% on stockbroking services); Zerodha charges page |
| Brokerage (Zerodha) | ₹0 delivery; intraday 0.03% or ₹20 per executed order, whichever is lower | each order | zerodha.com/charges (checked 31 Oct 2026) |
| DP charge (Zerodha) | ₹15.34 per scrip per day sold from demat (₹3.5 CDSL + ₹9.5 Zerodha + ₹2.34 GST) | sell | zerodha.com/charges |

## Tax on gains (resident individual, FY 2026-27; Budget 2026 left these unchanged)
| | Holding ≤ 12 months (STCG) | Holding > 12 months (LTCG) |
|---|---|---|
| Listed equity shares and equity-oriented ETFs (STT paid) | 20% | 12.5% on gains above ₹1.25 lakh per year |
| Gold/other non-equity ETFs | slab rate | 12.5% |
Plus 4% health and education cess (and surcharge at high incomes). From 1 Apr 2026 the sections are renumbered under the Income-tax Act, 2025, but the rates are the same. Sources: PIB Budget 2026 release; HSBC MF Tax Reckoner FY2026-27 (in 02_tax). This is a planning estimate, not tax advice.

## Things a contract note will teach you that this table can't
- How your broker rounds (STT is often rounded to the nearest rupee; check).
- Whether GST is shown as IGST 18% or CGST 9% + SGST 9% (it depends on your billing state).
- That DP charges are NOT on Zerodha's contract note: they're in the funds statement.
