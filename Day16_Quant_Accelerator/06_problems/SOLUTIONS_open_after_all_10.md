# Solutions
1. Results can't validate code. Real bugs often move the P&L by less than the noise, so a "reasonable-looking" number proves nothing. Only tests that pin behaviour do. *Correct-looking ≠ correct.*
2. With Faber flat at t, the sizer returns 0 before it ever computes vol, so the leak never runs. Fix: perturb with an always-in strategy, or test the component (the sizer) directly, at several t, and perturb ONLY bar t+1. *A test that can only fail when the market cooperates isn't a test.*
3. The cash constraint caps what fills, so held weight ≤ 1 even if the target is 1.76. Live, with margin (MTF) or a broker that allows it, the order goes in at 1.76×. *Test the intent, not the accident that hides it.*
4. A change that alters no observable behaviour, e.g. `self.cash > 0` → `>= 0` on line 166: interest on ₹0 is ₹0. Mark it, give the reason, move on. *Not every survivor is a hole; every survivor needs a verdict.*
5. Coverage says a line ran, not that its result was checked. `metrics()` runs in the demo but nothing asserts its output; `dd.min()` → `dd.max()` survives at 100% coverage. *Run ≠ checked.*
6. Equity [100, 110, 99, 121] on 4 dated days: assert max_dd = 99/110 − 1 = −10%, CAGR from the exact dates, vol and Sharpe against numpy by hand with the stated rf. *Hand-computable fixtures kill formula mutants.*
7. `with pytest.raises(RuntimeError): load_frame(csv_with_2019_01_01_row, ...)`, plus a 2018-12-31-only file that loads. It's the most expensive because a silent seal failure contaminates the OOS once and forever: no re-do on Day 20. *Guard the irreversible things first.*
8. H: interest accrues after the fill. P: on an exit day, cash after the close = pre-fill cash × growth + proceeds (no growth on proceeds). E: one-trade scenario at 10% p.a., frictionless, assert that identity to 1e-12. *Make the hypothesis able to lose.*
9. When there's a known good commit and a known bad one, and a test that tells them apart: bisect finds the breaking commit in log₂(n) steps. Today the "bad" version arrived as one file with no history, and diffing was banned. *Bisect needs history and a failing test; small commits give you both.*
10. No engine change merges without (a) a test that fails before it and passes after, (b) the full suite green, (c) mutation score not lower than before. *Tests are the contract; mutation score checks the contract.*
